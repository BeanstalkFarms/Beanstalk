## Verification

At [Arbitrum block 525,121,681](https://arbiscan.io/block/525121681), Beanstalk's owner was the BCM Safe, Beanstalk had BEAN's minter role, and the expected initializer address had no code. The exact batch generated by Safe Transaction Builder uses the Safe 1.3.0 MultiSendCallOnly deployment above. The batch succeeded in the BCM's `simulateAndRevert` call. A second read-only simulation asserted that both total BEAN supply and the receiving address's BEAN balance increased by exactly **79,000 BEAN**. The recipient had no deployed contract code on Arbitrum One. These checks do not exercise live Safe signatures, nonce selection, or any Safe transaction guard.

Selling **79,000 BEAN** at this block quoted **12,124.202034 USDC**, worth approximately **$12,121.71 USD**, via BEAN → wstETH → WETH → USDC. The quote includes route price impact and swap fees and excludes execution gas. This leaves approximately **$121.71** above the $12,000 fee. An exact-output quote for **12,002.464346 USDC**, sufficient for $12,000 at the observed USDC/USD rate of **0.99979468**, required **78,166.098923 BEAN**. The quote exceeds the fee by approximately 1%. A 2% decline from the quoted proceeds would fall below $12,000. Refresh before execution.
